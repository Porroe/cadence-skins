## Cadence 1.0

Initial release of Cadence skins for the **Innioasis Y2 running Y2Linux and Y2Reborn**.

- 14 skins: Blue, Purple, Red, Pink, Green, Yellow, and Orange in dark and light versions.
- Large readable fonts with 204 × 204 album artwork on Now Playing.
- Enlarged option values, consistent status icons, and readable list spacing.
- Includes fonts and their original license notices.

Download `Cadence-1.0.zip`, extract it, and copy your chosen Cadence folders into `Skins` on the player or SD card. Select the installed skin in Settings → Skin.

Based on Y2Reborn’s Big Type skin, with visual inspiration from [Adwaitapod Focus](https://github.com/Porroe/adwaitapod-focus).
